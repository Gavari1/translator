How En-i works

Consonant beginning: keep the opening consonant cluster together, insert en, and add i at the end.
Vowel beginning: place en before the word and i after it.
QU rule: the letters qu remain together as one opening pair.
Y rule: y counts as a vowel only when the word contains no a, e, i, o, or u.

Examples

crap → crenapi
shirt → shenirti
blue → blenuei
white → whenitei
quiet → quenieti
old → enoldi
thy → thenyi
I → eni · is → isi · a → ayi · the → thenei
