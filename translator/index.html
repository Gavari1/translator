Assumptions

Important: Brick length and height must be the actual physical brick dimensions, not nominal module dimensions that already include a mortar joint.
Intended for rectangular thin brick with reasonably uniform horizontal and vertical joints. Running bond and stack bond use the same area fraction. Irregular masonry, strongly varying joints, deeply tooled profiles, and field loss may require a different allowance or field verification.
